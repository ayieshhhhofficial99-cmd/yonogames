<h2>Body</h2>
<p>Smoke and flames rise from Los Angeles fatal helicopter crash site</p>
<p>Three people have died and two others taken to hospital after a news helicopter crashed in suburban Los Angeles, fire authorities in the city have said.</p>
<p>NBC Los Angeles confirmed the helicopter was its NewsChopper4, which also operated for Telemundo 52, and was in the area on Tuesday reporting on a deadly bus crash.</p>
<p>The station's hosts said both people on the helicopter, who were "valued friends and colleagues" had died, naming them as Eliana Moreno and George Marciniw.</p>
<p>They said the third victim was on the ground at the time of the crash, but did not name them.</p>
<p>The aircraft went down just before 19:00 local time (01:00 BST) between two commercial buildings in the Chatsworth neighbourhood, a Los Angeles Fire Department (LAFD) spokesperson told the BBC.</p>
<p>Fire from the crash spread to four vehicles and two storage containers, and more than 70 firefighters were deployed to the scene, the LAFD added.</p>
<p>During emotional live coverage on NBC4, veteran news anchor Colleen Williams confirmed it was the local news station's helicopter that crashed.</p>
<p>"We knew it was a news chopper. We knew we'd lost contact with our people out there, and we've just received confirmation," Williams said.</p>
<p>Earlier, LAFD Captain Branden Silverman told the local affiliate of the BBC's US partner CBS: "We do have one confirmed patient that seemed to be outside of the helicopter when it crashed.</p>
<p>"That person was declared deceased on scene," Silverman said.</p>
<p>Several news helicopters were in the area at the time of the incident reporting on a bus crash.</p>
<p>Earlier in the day, two people were killed and six others were injured after an SUV crashed into a city bus in Chatsworth, BBC's US partner CBS reported.</p>
<p>The helicopter crashed while covering a traffic collision involving an SUV and a city bus in Chatsworth</p>
<p>LAPD's Central Division posted on X that it extended "thoughts and prayers to the family, friends and colleagues of KNBC 4, Los Angeles".</p>
<p>Los Angeles Mayor Karen Bass wrote on X she was "absolutely devastated by the loss of life in Chatsworth tonight".</p>
<p>"My heart is with the families and loved ones of those we lost in the tragic bus collision and helicopter crash," she added.</p>
<p>California Governor Gavin Newsom wrote on X that "our hearts are with the victims and families of tonight's devastating Metro bus and helicopter crashes in Chatsworth, and with the entire NBC4 family".</p>
<p>"Thank you to the first responders working tirelessly tonight."</p>
<p>The National Transportation Safety Board (NTSB) will lead the investigation into the incident.</p>
