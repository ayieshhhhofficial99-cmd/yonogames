<h2>Body</h2>
<p>Loop any section forever · hit «Next» · the music evolves — seamlessly, on the beat</p>
<p>In games, music reacts : stay in the tavern and its theme loops forever; descend into</p>
<p>the dungeon and the score darkens with you. musslop does this with any mp3.</p>
<p>Drop a track → AI finds the musical structure → every section becomes a perfect loop →</p>
<p>you drive the music live, like a game audio engine — no editing skills required.</p>
<p>The flagship use case is tabletop RPG : turn any track into a location theme .</p>
<p>The party lingers in the tavern — the tavern section loops. They open the dungeon door —</p>
<p>one keypress, and the music descends with them, on the beat, mid-session.</p>
<p>There is even a Tavern UI theme (wood, parchment &amp; gold) switchable in the header.</p>
<p>Also great for: game dev prototyping (audition adaptive behaviour before wiring</p>
<p>FMOD/Wwise, export loop WAVs), streaming, practice looping, focus music.</p>
<p>Windows (needs Python 3.10+ with "Add to PATH" checked):</p>
<p>ffmpeg is downloaded automatically with the dependencies — no manual install needed.</p>
<p>Optional AI engines (each is one command, both auto-detected by run.sh ):</p>
<p>The All-In-One engine is not supported natively on Windows (its NATTEN</p>
<p>dependency needs a source build); use WSL2 with the Linux script if you</p>
<p>need it. SongFormer covers structure analysis fully, and the stem</p>
<p>"layers" feature also works through it on Windows.</p>
<p>Works fully offline after setup. First AI analysis of a track: ~1 min on GPU,</p>
<p>a few minutes on CPU; results are cached — reopening is instant.</p>
<p>Built on the shoulders of: SongFormer (ASLP-lab, CC-BY-4.0) · All-In-One (Kim &amp; Nam, MIT) · Beat This!</p>
<p>(CPJKU, MIT) · Demucs · librosa —</p>
<p>plus classic MIR: Ellis 2007 (beat tracking), Foote 2000 (novelty segmentation).</p>
