However, in global assessments of climate change, report authors must look further upstream in the process, because the use of AI tools can change a report’s findings before a single word is written. In March, the Intergovernmental Panel on Climate Change (IPCC) announced that it was developing AI support for literature reviews and processing reviewer comments (see go.nature.com/4ibf4d2 ). These tools could alter which evidence becomes visible, how studies are grouped and which signals reach authors’ attention.</p>
