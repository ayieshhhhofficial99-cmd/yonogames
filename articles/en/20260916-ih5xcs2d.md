<p>India on Wednesday summoned Pakistan's top diplomat to protest what it called "unacceptable and unprofessional conduct" by Pakistani naval units that led to a collision with an Indian warship in the Arabian Sea, India's Foreign Ministry said in a statement.</p>
<p>According to India, a Pakistani naval ship approached its warship at high speed, causing a collision on Tuesday in international waters. No major damage was reported.</p>
<p>The Indian ship was on a routine surveillance mission, officials said.</p>
<p>"The Charge d'Affaires of Pakistan was advised to ​convey to the concerned Pakistani authorities, the need for all military units to observe due care and respect the provisions ​of the relevant agreements between the ‌two sides in order to prevent any repeat of such incidents," the ministry said.</p>
<p>India said the incident violated a 1991 agreement requiring the countries' naval vessels and submarines to maintain a distance of at least three nautical miles in international waters.</p>
<p>Pakistan has not issued a statement regarding the incident.</p>
<p>This incident marks the first overt military tension between the nuclear-armed rivals since their four-day conflict last year, which threatened to turn into a full-blown war .</p>
<p>India-US ties could come under pressure as the US lawmakers consider a Russia sanctions bill that would allow President Donald Trump to impose tariffs of up to 100% on countries buying Russian oil and gas. India, a major consumer of Russian energy, could be affected by the measure.</p>
<p>The Lindsey O. Graham Sanctioning Russia and Iran Act of 2026 cleared a key procedural hurdle in the House of Representatives on Tuesday in a 214-211 vote, media reports said. The House is expected to hold a final vote on Wednesday.</p>
<p>The bill, imposing sanctions on Russia's leadership, energy and defense sectors and "shadow fleet" accused of helping Moscow evade oil sanctions, was passed by the Senate last month.</p>
<p>If approved by the House, the bill would go to Trump to be signed into law.</p>
<p>The proposed tariffs seek to increase economic pressure on Moscow by targeting countries that continue buying Russian energy.</p>
<p>While India and Russia ties have grown stronger since the start of the war in Ukraine, driven largely by Russian oil exports, the same cannot be said of India-US relations under Prime Minister Narendra Modi as tensions mounted over New Delhi's purchases of Russian oil .</p>
<p>Last August, Trump imposed an additional 25% tariff on Indian imports over those purchases, on top of a 25% reciprocal tariff.</p>
<p>However, the additional Russia-related tariff was removed in February 2026, while the reciprocal tariff was reduced to 18% as part of a US-India trade framework.</p>
<p>However, the landmark bilateral trade agreement between the two countries remains in limbo, with no significant breakthrough.</p>
